(02)Next: Edition 02

Edition 02: cotton down to the thread.

A

One fiber

Every Edition 02 garment will be 100% cotton, down to the thread, label, drawcord and rib trim, so it can be shredded and spun into new yarn.

B

Cotton thread

Patent pending

Cotton thread is usually too weak for apparel seams, so the industry sews cotton clothes with polyester. Our patent-pending stitching method holds cotton seams together with cotton thread, so Edition 02 will have nothing to cut out before recycling.

C

Prints that recycle

Edition 02 dark pieces will be printed with water-based discharge ink, which forms the design by lifting the garment's dye instead of laying plastic on top. Light pieces will use a bio-based algae ink. No plastisol, so prints go through mechanical recycling with the garment.

D

Every piece traced

Each Edition 02 piece will have a unique code woven into its cotton label. Scan it with any phone to see who made it, what it is made from, and where it goes when you are done. No chips, nothing to remove before recycling.

Read the label closely

Most "100% cotton" clothing isn't.

Under US labeling rules, sewing thread, trims, cuffs, waistbands and labels don't count toward a garment's fiber content. So a shirt can be labeled 100% cotton while being sewn with polyester thread, finished with spandex rib and printed with plastic ink. It's legal, but it means the garment can't go straight into cotton recycling: the seams and prints have to be cut away, or the plastic ends up in the recycled fiber.

Edition 01 is made this standard way too. Edition 02 will be cotton in every part that touches the recycling process, so the whole garment can be shredded and spun into new yarn.

No chips, no GPS, no location tracking. The only ID is a code woven into the cotton label.
