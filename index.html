(02)Principles

Made to keep, made to trace, made to come back.

A

One fiber

Every garment is 100% cotton, down to the thread, label, drawcord and rib trim, so it can be shredded and spun into new yarn.

B

Cotton thread

Patent pending

Cotton thread is usually too weak for apparel seams, so the industry sews cotton clothes with polyester. Our stitching method holds cotton seams together with cotton thread, so there is nothing to cut out before recycling.

C

Prints that recycle

Dark pieces are printed with water-based discharge ink, which forms the design by lifting the garment's dye instead of laying plastic on top. Light pieces use a bio-based algae ink, also water-based. No plastisol anywhere, so prints go through mechanical recycling with the garment.

D

Built to keep

Heavyweight fabric and repairs on request, made to be worn for years and passed on.

E

Every piece traced

Every piece has a unique code woven into its cotton label. Scan it with any phone to see who made it, what it is made from, and where it goes when you are done. No chips, nothing to remove before recycling.

Read the label closely

Most "100% cotton" clothing isn't.

Under US labeling rules, sewing thread, trims, cuffs, waistbands and labels don't count toward a garment's fiber content. So a shirt can be labeled 100% cotton while being sewn with polyester thread, finished with spandex rib and printed with plastic ink. It's legal, but it means the garment can't go straight into cotton recycling: the seams and prints have to be cut away, or the plastic ends up in the recycled fiber.

Ours is cotton in every part that touches the recycling process, so the whole garment can be shredded and spun into new yarn.

(05)Privacy

Your clothes don't track you. They carry their own history.

No chips, no GPS, no location tracking. The only ID is a code woven into the cotton label.

Nothing is recorded unless you choose to scan it. The code links to the garment's record, not to you.

When a piece changes hands, the previous owner's data stays with them.
